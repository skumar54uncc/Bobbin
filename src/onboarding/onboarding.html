Welcome to Bobbin

The missing memory for your AI.

Before Bobbin indexes anything. Bobbin builds a private library of readable pages you open in this browser. That includes page text, titles, and URLs stored only on this device. Sensitive sites (banking, health, email, login pages) and Incognito windows are skipped.

If you allow it below, Bobbin will also scan up to 500 pages from your last 30 days of browser history to seed the library, and will index open tabs. You can pause indexing, block sites, or forget data anytime in Settings.

Optional Assistant Sync (Google Drive) and Cloud Chat (Gemini) stay off until you enable them separately in Settings. With Assistant Sync on, the text of pages you read syncs to your own Google Drive. LinkedIn feed post text syncs only if you turn that setting on.

How it works

  1. 1

    Browse as usual

    After you start indexing, Bobbin remembers readable pages as you go. Pause indexing or block sites anytime in Settings.

  2. 2

    Open Search or Ask

    Double-tap Shift, or click the toolbar icon. On a normal site the panel opens over the page. On Chrome's own pages it opens in the side panel.

  3. 3

    Find what you read

    Search by meaning or chat with your library. Optional Cloud Chat uses Google Gemini only if you turn it on in Settings.
