Bobbin

The missing memory for your AI.

Read it once. Ask your AI anytime.

Bobbin keeps the thread of everything you read.

Free forever. No ads. No servers.

Bobbin is a private Chrome extension that remembers pages you read on this device so you can search them, ask questions, and optionally share that memory with your own AI assistant through your Google Drive. Indexing, search, and the language model run on your computer. There is no Bobbin server, no account, and no tracking.

Install from the Chrome Web Store Send feedback

The store listing link will point at Bobbin's item page when the listing is live. Until then, that button opens the Chrome Web Store home.

What Bobbin remembers

  • Articles and pages you read, after you press I agree on the welcome page
  • YouTube videos you watch, from their captions
  • PDFs you open, with links to the right page
  • Data tables, row by row
  • LinkedIn profiles you viewed

Optional Assistant Sync

Off until you press I agree. Copies the same memory into a folder named Bobbin Memory in your own Google Drive. The Google permission is drive.file: files Bobbin creates, not your whole Drive.
